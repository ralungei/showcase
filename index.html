Frequently asked

Why does Showcase ask for Screen Recording permission?

It is the core of the app: recording your own screen. macOS requires you to grant it once in System Settings, Privacy & Security.

Why does it ask for Accessibility permission?

It is optional. While recording, Showcase timestamps your clicks and keystrokes so the editor can add click effects, typing sounds, and typing speed-ups afterwards. That data stays inside your project file, on your Mac. Recording works without it.

Where are my projects stored?

As portable .showcase files on your Mac. You can copy them to another Mac and keep editing, and open them from File > Open Recent or by double clicking.
