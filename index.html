Frequently asked

Showcase is now Screenie. Do I need to do anything?

No. It is the same app with a new name. Your projects, settings and permissions carry over as they are.

Why does Screenie ask for Screen Recording permission?

It is the core of the app: recording your own screen. macOS requires you to grant it once in System Settings, Privacy & Security.

Why does it ask for the Camera and Microphone?

Both are optional. They let you include your webcam and your voice in a recording, and record new camera or voice takes in the editor. Everything is recorded on your Mac. Recording your screen works without them.

Why does it ask for Accessibility permission?

It is optional. While recording, Screenie timestamps your clicks and your typing so the editor can add click effects, typing sounds, and typing speed-ups afterwards. That data stays inside your project file, on your Mac. Recording works without it.

How are subtitles made?

With the speech recognition built into macOS. On Macs that support it, transcription runs entirely on your Mac. Speech Recognition permission is only requested the first time you generate subtitles.

What do I need for the AI Editor?

It is optional and uses your own Google Gemini API key, which you can create for free at Google AI Studio. Your requests go straight from your Mac to Google. Without a key, the AI features stay off and everything else works offline. Voice enhancement works the same way with your own ElevenLabs key.

Where are my projects stored?

As portable project files on your Mac (they keep the .showcase extension, so older projects open as before). You can copy them to another Mac and keep editing, and open them from File > Open Recent or by double clicking.
