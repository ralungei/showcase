Privacy Policy

Showcase for macOS · Last updated July 2026

The short version

Showcase collects no data. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no tracking, and no network communication. Everything you record and edit stays on your Mac.

What the app stores locally

  • Your screen recordings, saved as project files on your Mac.
  • Optionally, timestamps of your own clicks and keystrokes during a recording, used only to render click effects, typing sounds, and typing speed-ups in your video. This metadata lives inside the project file and never leaves your device.
  • Your app preferences (cursor style, sound choices, and similar), stored in the standard macOS preferences location.

What we collect

Nothing. Showcase sends no data to us or to anyone else. We have no servers.

System permissions

  • Screen Recording: required to record your screen. Granted and revocable in System Settings.
  • Microphone: only if you choose to record your voice.
  • Accessibility: optional, used to capture your own clicks and keystrokes as local metadata for editing effects.

Changes and contact

If this policy ever changes, the update will be published on this page. Questions: ras.alungei@gmail.com.
